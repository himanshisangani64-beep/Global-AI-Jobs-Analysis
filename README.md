## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to analyze the global Artificial Intelligence job market for 2026 using a synthetic dataset containing 5,000 job records.

The dashboard explores AI specializations, salaries across countries, work-from-home opportunities, experience-level salary distribution, job growth, industry-wise job openings, and automation risk by job title.

It combines data analysis, KPI cards, charts, and interactive company filters to transform raw job data into meaningful insights about the AI job market.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze global AI job market trends.
- Understand salary distribution across AI specializations and countries.
- Identify AI specializations with high growth projections.
- Compare job openings across different industries.
- Analyze salary differences across experience levels.
- Examine work-from-home opportunities and job openings.
- Explore automation risk across AI job titles.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for data-driven career insights.

## ✨ Key Features

- Total AI jobs KPI
- Average salary KPI
- Job satisfaction KPI
- Total job openings KPI
- AI specialization-wise salary analysis
- Country-wise salary comparison
- Work-from-home job opening analysis
- Experience-level salary distribution
- Top AI specializations by growth projection
- Industry-wise job opening comparison
- Job title-wise automation risk analysis
- Interactive company selection filter
- Multiple charts and KPI cards
- Consolidated dashboard for AI job market analysis

## 📊 Analysis & Excel Practice

### 1. 💰 Salary Analysis

Compared salaries across AI specializations and countries to understand compensation differences in the AI job market.

### 2. 🌎 Country-wise Analysis

Analyzed salary values across countries, including India, Brazil, Japan, France, the United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, and the United States.

### 3. 🧠 AI Specialization Analysis

Compared specializations such as Robotics, Generative AI, AI Research, Data Engineering, Machine Learning, and other AI-related fields.

### 4. 📈 Job Growth Analysis

Identified leading AI specializations based on their displayed job growth projections.

### 5. 🏢 Industry-wise Job Openings

Compared job openings across industries such as Healthcare, Technology, Finance, Automotive, and Retail.

### 6. 🏠 Work-from-Home Analysis

Compared job openings for work-from-home availability to understand remote work opportunities.

### 7. 👨‍💻 Experience-level Salary Analysis

Visualized salary distribution across Executive, Lead, Senior, Mid-level, and Entry-level experience categories.

### 8. ⚙️ Automation Risk Analysis

Compared automation risk percentages across AI-related job titles to explore differences in estimated automation exposure.

### 9. 📊 Dashboard & Data Visualization

Combined KPI cards, bar charts, a pie chart, and interactive company filters into a single dashboard for clear and efficient analysis.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the values displayed in the dashboard:

- **Total AI Jobs:** 5,000 job records.
- **Average Salary:** Approximately $134,448, as displayed on the dashboard.
- **Total Job Openings:** 44,638.
- **Job Satisfaction:** 7.11.
- **Highest Salary by Country:** United States, at approximately $164,219.
- **Lowest Salary by Country:** India, at approximately $58,563.
- **Top AI Specialization by Growth:** Generative AI, with 39.57% growth.
- **Industry with the Most Job Openings:** Healthcare, with 50 openings in the displayed chart.
- **Work-from-Home Opportunities:** 293 job openings marked Yes and 105 marked No in the displayed chart.
- **Experience-level Salary Distribution:** The dashboard compares salaries across five experience levels.
- **Automation Risk:** The dashboard compares automation risk across different AI job titles.

*Note: These insights reflect the dashboard's displayed values. The dataset is synthetic and should not be interpreted as verified real-world employment statistics.*
